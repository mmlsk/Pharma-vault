# Nootropics

**Scope:** Cognitive enhancers, wakefulness agents, racetams, cholinergics, adaptogens and CNS-active medicines from neurology and psychiatry.

## Interpretation
Most "nootropic" effects are conditional — they compensate for sleep loss, fatigue or deficiency rather than raising baseline cognition in rested healthy people. Stimulant, cholinergic and serotonergic overlap are the dominant stacking risks.
