# Growth Hormone and Peptide Agents

**Scope:** Somatropin, GH secretagogues (GHRH analogues, ghrelin mimetics), research peptides and FGFR3/growth-plate agents.

## Interpretation
Many products are alternate entries into the same GH–IGF-1 axis, so stacking them is mostly redundancy. Research peptides frequently lack defined composition and human safety data.
