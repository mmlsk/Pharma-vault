# Metabolic and Incretin Agents

**Scope:** GLP-1 receptor agonists, dual/triple incretin agonists and body-composition agents.

## Interpretation
Clinically the best-grounded cluster (outcome trials for several agents), but grey-market sourcing, compounded products and combination with other anorectics or insulin secretagogues introduce hypoglycaemia, dehydration/AKI, pancreatitis/biliary and identity risks.
