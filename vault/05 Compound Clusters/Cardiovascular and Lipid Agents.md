# Cardiovascular and Lipid Agents

**Scope:** Antihypertensives, lipid-lowering agents, antiplatelets/anticoagulants, nitrates and PDE-related vascular agents.

## Interpretation
Relevant both as treatments and as interaction partners — e.g. nitrate + PDE5 inhibitor hypotension, additive QT or bleeding risk, CYP3A4-dependent statin exposure.
