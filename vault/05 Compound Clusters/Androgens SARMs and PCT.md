# Androgens SARMs and PCT

**Scope:** Anabolic-androgenic steroids, SARMs, SERMs, aromatase inhibitors, antiandrogens and post-cycle agents.

## Interpretation
Treat as one feedback-regulated system: exogenous androgens suppress the HPG axis, and 'PCT' agents act on the same feedback loop. Cardiovascular (lipids, haematocrit, LVH), hepatic (17α-alkylated orals) and psychiatric risks dominate.
