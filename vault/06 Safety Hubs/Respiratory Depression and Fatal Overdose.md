# Respiratory Depression and Fatal Overdose

> [!warning] Educational content — not medical advice, no dosing guidance.

Highest-severity hub.

## Contributors
Opioids (including novel nitazenes, O-desmethyltramadol, high-dose kratom alkaloids), benzodiazepines and designer benzodiazepines, gabapentinoids, phenibut-like GABA-B agents, barbiturates, GHB/GBL, alcohol, sedating dissociatives and antihistamines.

## Mechanism of harm
Additive CNS and brainstem respiratory depression — particularly **opioid + benzodiazepine/gabapentinoid/alcohol** combinations. Designer benzodiazepines and nitazenes have unpredictable potency and are frequent adulterants.

## Related risks
Dependence and withdrawal (benzodiazepine/phenibut/alcohol withdrawal seizures), aspiration, anterograde amnesia.

Nitazene analogues are recorded here as forensic/high-risk entries — never as biohacking candidates.
