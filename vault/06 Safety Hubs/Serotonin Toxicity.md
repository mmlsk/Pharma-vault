# Serotonin Toxicity

> [!warning] Educational content — not medical advice, no dosing guidance.

High-severity hub for **multi-drug serotonergic accumulation** (serotonin syndrome).

## Contributors
MAO inhibitors (including linezolid and methylene blue), SSRIs/SNRIs, TCAs (clomipramine), serotonin releasers (MDMA and analogues), tramadol, pethidine, fentanyl, dextromethorphan, triptans (low absolute risk), lithium, St John's wort, 5-HTP/tryptophan, serotonergic psychedelics.

## Clinical picture
Triad of altered mental status, autonomic instability (hyperthermia, tachycardia, diaphoresis) and neuromuscular excitation (clonus — inducible/ocular, hyperreflexia, rigidity). Hunter criteria are used for diagnosis. Onset usually within hours of adding or escalating an agent.

## Reading rule
MAOI combinations are the highest-risk pattern; washout periods matter (e.g. long-half-life fluoxetine).
