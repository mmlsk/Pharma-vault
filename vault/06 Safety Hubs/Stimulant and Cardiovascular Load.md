# Stimulant and Cardiovascular Load

> [!warning] Educational content — not medical advice, no dosing guidance.

Risk hub for **sympathomimetic, wake-promoting and haemodynamic burden** — the most common "hidden bill" of aggressive enhancement stacks.

## Contributors
Amphetamines, methylphenidate and analogues, modafinil/armodafinil and analogues, caffeine and other methylxanthines, ephedrine/synephrine/yohimbine, beta-2 agonists used off-label (clenbuterol), thyroid hormone misuse, anabolic steroids (lipids, haematocrit, LVH), synthetic cathinones.

## Mechanism of harm
Tachycardia, hypertension, vasoconstriction, arrhythmia, reduced sleep (see [[Sleep-Wake Regulation]]), hyperthermia; for androgens — dyslipidaemia, erythrocytosis, ventricular remodelling.

## Key interactions
- MAO inhibitors + sympathomimetics → hypertensive crisis
- Multiple stimulants → additive BP/HR and insomnia
- QT-prolonging combinations; hypokalaemia (beta-agonists, diuretics) lowers the arrhythmia threshold
- Nitrate + PDE5 inhibitor → profound hypotension (absolute contraindication)

## Monitoring (educational)
BP, HR, ECG where indicated, lipids, haematocrit, sleep.
