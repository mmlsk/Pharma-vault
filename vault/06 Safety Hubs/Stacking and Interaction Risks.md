# Stacking and Interaction Risks

> [!warning] Educational content — not medical advice, no dosing guidance.

The umbrella hub. It is framed around **overlap**, not around "building the perfect stack". Before combining any two agents, screen these five dimensions:

| Dimension | Question | Typical failure |
|---|---|---|
| Duplicated mechanism | Do both act on the same receptor, transporter or axis? | [[Stack Redundancy]] — more side-effects, no more benefit |
| Overlapping adverse-effect domain | Do both raise the same risk (BP, QT, sedation, serotonin, seizure threshold)? | Additive toxicity even without a PK interaction |
| Pharmacokinetic interaction | Does one inhibit/induce the other's clearance (CYP, P-gp, renal)? | Exposure changes several-fold — see [[CYP450 Metabolism]] |
| Contraindication | Is there a labelled absolute contraindication? | e.g. nitrate + PDE5 inhibitor, MAOI + serotonergic/sympathomimetic agent |
| Monitoring burden | Does the combination require labs, ECG or BP follow-up? | Silent harm (lipids, haematocrit, LFTs, electrolytes) |
