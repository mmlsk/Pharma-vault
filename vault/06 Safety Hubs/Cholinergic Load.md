# Cholinergic Load

> [!warning] Educational content — not medical advice, no dosing guidance.

Mechanism hub for **acetylcholine supply + breakdown inhibition + direct receptor agonism**.

## Contributors
- Choline donors: citicoline (CDP-choline), alpha-GPC (choline alfoscerate), choline bitartrate
- Acetylcholinesterase inhibitors: huperzine A, galantamine, donepezil, rivastigmine
- Direct/allosteric agonists and modulators: nicotinic (e.g. GTS-21) and muscarinic agents
- Racetams are often *marketed* as requiring choline — this is a community heuristic, not an established pharmacological requirement.

## Why it matters
Combining several donors with an AChE inhibitor is a classic overshoot pattern. Excess cholinergic tone → headache, GI upset (nausea, diarrhoea, cramps), hypersalivation, sweating, **bradycardia**, AV-block risk, bronchospasm; in severe cases a cholinergic crisis.

## Clinical cautions
Bradyarrhythmia / conduction disease, asthma/COPD, active peptic ulcer, seizure disorders, urinary or GI obstruction; interactions with beta-blockers and other bradycardic drugs, and with anticholinergic medicines (functional antagonism).
