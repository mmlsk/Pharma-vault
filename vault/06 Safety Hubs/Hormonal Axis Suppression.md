# Hormonal Axis Suppression

> [!warning] Educational content — not medical advice, no dosing guidance.

Treats androgens, SARMs, SERMs, gonadotropins, aromatase inhibitors, GnRH agents and GH-axis agents as **one feedback-regulated system**, not isolated compounds.

## Core physiology
Exogenous androgens and androgenic SARMs suppress hypothalamic GnRH and pituitary LH/FSH → reduced endogenous testosterone and spermatogenesis. Aromatase inhibitors and SERMs act on the estradiol arm of the same loop; hCG bypasses the pituitary. See [[HPG and GH-IGF-1 Axes]].

## Risks
Hypogonadism after cessation, infertility, gynecomastia, low-estradiol effects (bone, lipids, mood), dyslipidaemia, erythrocytosis, hepatotoxicity with 17α-alkylated orals, mood and psychiatric effects. GH-axis agents add insulin resistance and oedema.

## Reading rule
"PCT" protocols are community constructs; evidence for reliable axis recovery with them is limited. Recovery time is variable.
