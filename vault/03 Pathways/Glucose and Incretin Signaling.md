# Glucose and Incretin Signaling

GLP-1 and GIP potentiate glucose-dependent insulin secretion, slow gastric emptying and reduce appetite centrally. Metformin lowers hepatic gluconeogenesis (AMPK-linked); SGLT2 inhibitors increase glucosuria with cardiorenal benefit. Key risks: hypoglycaemia with insulin/sulfonylureas, GI effects and dehydration, euglycaemic ketoacidosis (SGLT2i).
