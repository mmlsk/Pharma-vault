# Catecholamines and Attention

Dopamine and noradrenaline regulate vigilance, working memory and reward (prefrontal D1/α2A signalling follows an inverted-U curve). Stimulants act via DAT/NET inhibition or release; modafinil mainly via weak DAT inhibition plus orexin/histamine effects; tyrosine is a precursor with conditional effects under stress.
