# Serotonergic Signaling

5-HT synthesis from tryptophan (TPH, rate-limiting), reuptake by SERT, degradation by MAO-A. 5-HT1A (anxiolysis/autoreceptor), 5-HT2A (psychedelic effects, plasticity), 5-HT2B (valvulopathy concern with chronic agonists), 5-HT3 (emesis).

Risk hub: [[Serotonin Toxicity]]
