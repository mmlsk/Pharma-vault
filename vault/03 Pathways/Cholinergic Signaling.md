# Cholinergic Signaling

Acetylcholine synthesis (choline + acetyl-CoA via ChAT), release, and hydrolysis by acetylcholinesterase. Nicotinic (ionotropic) and muscarinic M1–M5 (GPCR) receptors. Enhancement strategies: precursor loading, AChE inhibition, receptor agonism/positive allosteric modulation.
