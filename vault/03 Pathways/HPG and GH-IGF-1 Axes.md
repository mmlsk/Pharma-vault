# HPG and GH-IGF-1 Axes

**HPG:** GnRH → LH/FSH → testosterone/estradiol with negative feedback at hypothalamus and pituitary. **GH–IGF-1:** GHRH (+) and somatostatin (−) regulate pulsatile GH; ghrelin receptor agonists amplify pulses; hepatic IGF-1 feeds back negatively. Secretagogues, somatropin and IGF-1 are alternate entries into the same axis — see [[Stack Redundancy]].
