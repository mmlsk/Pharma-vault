# Sleep-Wake Regulation

Two-process model: homeostatic sleep pressure (adenosine accumulation) and circadian rhythm. Wake-promoting systems: orexin/hypocretin, histamine (H3 inverse agonists such as pitolisant), noradrenaline, dopamine. Caffeine blocks adenosine A1/A2A receptors — masking sleep pressure rather than repaying it.

Central concept: many wakefulness compounds buy performance back only under deficit.
