# CYP450 Metabolism

Phase I oxidation, dominated by CYP3A4/5 (~half of marketed drugs), CYP2D6, CYP2C9, CYP2C19 and CYP1A2. Strong inhibitors (e.g. clarithromycin, ketoconazole/itraconazole, ritonavir, grapefruit juice for gut CYP3A4) raise substrate exposure; inducers (rifampicin, carbamazepine, St John's wort) lower it. Prodrugs invert the consequence. Transporters (P-gp, OATP1B1) interact with the same drugs.

Polymorphisms: CYP2D6 and CYP2C19 poor/ultrarapid metabolisers.
