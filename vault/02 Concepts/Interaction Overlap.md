# Interaction Overlap

Interaction burden is broader than direct receptor overlap. It includes:

1. **Shared pharmacodynamics** — same target or same downstream physiology.
2. **Shared risk** — different mechanisms converging on one adverse outcome (QT, BP, sedation, seizure threshold, serotonin load).
3. **Shared metabolism** — enzyme/transporter competition, inhibition or induction ([[CYP450 Metabolism]]).
4. **Shared physiology** — sleep debt, dehydration, renal or hepatic reserve.
