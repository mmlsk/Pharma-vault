Reading guide
Mechanism hubs show where compounds converge on the same pathway.Useful for spotting redundancy, not just benefits.
Risk nodes collect additive harms like insomnia, cholinergic overload, endocrine suppression, hypotension, and CYP interactions.These are often more practically important than the “benefit” nodes.
Longevity and peptide clusters are visually separated because the source repeatedly treats them as mechanistically interesting but clinically incomplete.That distinction is part of the message.
